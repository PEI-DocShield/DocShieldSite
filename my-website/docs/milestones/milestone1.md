## Context

- **Large volumes of documents:** Organizations handle extensive collections of documents containing personal and sensitive data.

- **Growing need for effective and secure anonymization:** The increasing circulation of personal information makes reliable anonymization essential.

- **Limitations of current solutions:** Existing tools often fall short in terms of efficiency, flexibility, and overall scope.


## Problem

- **Difficulty anonymizing large volumes quickly:** Processing many documents manually can be slow and difficult to scale.

- **Slow, error-prone manual drafting:** Manual work requires significant effort and can lead to inconsistencies or missed sensitive information.

- **Existing tools limited to a few formats:** Many available solutions support only a restricted range of document types.

- **Incomplete solutions:** Current tools do not always provide an end-to-end workflow, from document analysis to secure anonymization.

## Goals

- **Practical and comprehensive solution:** Develop an automatic anonymization platform that is useful, accessible, and complete.

- **Support for multiple formats and documents:** Allow users to process different document types and large volumes efficiently.

- **Application of various anonymization techniques:** Combine different methods, including reversible anonymization when necessary.

- **Fast and reliable processing:** Ensure efficient results while respecting applicable legislation and data protection requirements.

- **Multilingual support and selective anonymization:** Support multiple languages and allow users to choose specific areas for anonymization.
